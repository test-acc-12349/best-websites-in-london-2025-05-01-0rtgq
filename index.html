Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a custom website?

Most projects are completed within 4-8 weeks, depending on complexity. We follow a structured timeline: 1 week for planning, 2-3 weeks for design, 2-3 weeks for development, and 1 week for testing and launch.

What's included in your website packages?

Our comprehensive packages include custom design, responsive development, SEO optimization, content management system, security features, analytics integration, and 30 days of post-launch support.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance and support?

Yes, we offer flexible maintenance packages including regular updates, security monitoring, backup management, and technical support. Our team is available 24/7 for urgent issues.

How do you ensure website security?

We implement multiple security layers including SSL certificates, regular security audits, malware scanning, and automated backup systems. All our websites comply with latest data protection regulations.

What makes your websites different?

Our websites combine stunning design with powerful functionality. We focus on conversion optimization, fast loading speeds, and user experience. Each site is custom-built to meet your specific business goals.
